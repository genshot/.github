# Genshot

**Store-ready listing images from your real app UI.**

Genshot helps indie developers turn the screenshots they already have—or a public
App Store link—into polished, reviewable listing images for the App Store, Chrome
Web Store, and Google Play. No design tool required.

## Start free

Every new account includes 10 free generations. Open
[genshot.dev](https://genshot.dev) to sign in, add your screenshots or store link,
and generate your first set.

## Build with Genshot

- [API documentation](https://genshot.dev/api-docs) — generate from your product,
  CI workflow, or coding agent.
- [@genshot/cli](https://github.com/genshot/cli) — the public command-line tool
  for App Store, Chrome Web Store, and Google Play listing images.

Your original UI stays visible throughout the process, so every image is
reviewable before it reaches your store listing.
